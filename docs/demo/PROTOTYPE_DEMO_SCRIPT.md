## Narration

Hi, this is Longview. It’s a mobile Web app for those times when you’re trying to make progress on several big goals at once. What you’re seeing today is our interactive prototype, so let me show you the experience we’re building.

The problem with most planning tools is that they give every goal its own list, as if your time were unlimited. Longview starts with the time you actually have. In this example, I have twelve hours this week, split between building a SaaS startup, learning applied AI, and preparing to build a house. I can immediately see what I’m prioritizing, and what I’m choosing not to prioritize yet.

Now there’s a conflict. My startup interviews and my AI evaluation both want the same morning. Clara looks across my goals, my available time, and the decisions I’ve already made. She suggests keeping the customer interview and moving the more flexible AI task to Wednesday.

But Clara doesn’t just go ahead and change my calendar. First, she shows me exactly what will move, where it will go, and why she thinks that’s the better tradeoff. I can apply the change, adjust it, or cancel it. I stay in control.

Once I approve it, Today updates with the new plan. Longview also keeps the reason behind that change, so I don’t have to reconstruct my thinking a week later. And if I tap twice, the same task won’t get moved twice.

Longview keeps research connected to the goal too. Clara can bring me useful evidence, but I decide what to accept, reject, or save for later. Anything I accept becomes a proposed update to the Goal Brief. I can still edit it before it becomes part of the plan.

And when I finally reach a goal, Longview helps me capture what worked. I choose what Clara is allowed to remember and reuse next time. Nothing private is carried forward without my permission.

We’ve also designed for the messy moments. If I’m offline, my work stays clearly marked as waiting to sync. If Clara times out or returns something invalid, my last approved plan stays exactly as it was.

That’s Longview: a way to pursue several ambitions without pretending they all fit at once. Clara helps me think across the whole picture, but I make every decision that actually changes my plan.
